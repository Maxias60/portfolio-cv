# About Me

I have always been interested in **how things work**. What started with building electronic LEGO Technic models and dismantling old electronics gradually evolved into a broader interest in **mechanical design, electronics, programming, and robotics**.

One of my first major technical projects was building a **railgun** for my pre-university (VWO) final project. This introduced me to soldering, electronics, and embedded programming using **Arduino** and, later, the **ESP32**. Since then, I have primarily expanded my knowledge through hands-on building.

Over the years, I have completed a wide range of projects, from **high-performance custom e-bikes** to **3D-printed parts** and mechanical designs. Building e-bikes, in particular, drove me to develop skills in:

<ul>
  <li>CAD and mechanical design</li>
  <li>
    - Electronics, battery systems, and hands-on experience with high-power BLDC motor systems
  </li>
  <li>Embedded programming</li>
  <li>3D printing and manufacturing</li>
  <li>3D scanning and reverse engineering</li>
</ul>

Along the way, I earned my **Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional (CSWP)** certification, learned to work with **Onshape**, and gained experience using **3D scanning** as part of my prototyping and reverse-engineering workflow.

These projects strengthened my interest in **multidisciplinary engineering**, especially projects that combine mechanics, electronics, and software.

I enjoy transforming ideas into **functional prototypes**, understanding the systems behind them, and continuously improving designs through testing and iteration.

This portfolio is a collection of both **academic and personal projects** that reflect that process.
